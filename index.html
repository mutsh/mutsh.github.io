DealerFlow AI

A service-booking agent for car-dealership workshops, working over web chat, SMS and WhatsApp. The language model only interprets the request; pricing, availability and bookings come from deterministic tools, every booking needs the customer's explicit yes, and safety complaints go straight to a person. Includes a manager dashboard, PII-redacted tracing, CI tests and an Azure deployment plan.

FastAPI, Qwen 2.5 (optional local router), Twilio, Odoo CRM, Docker, GitHub Actions, Azure

How I deliver AI

Most AI projects stall between demo and daily use. I run them like the enterprise programmes I led for eight years: clear scope, controlled risk, measured outcomes.

4 large-scale programmesDigital transformation and automation, delivered with Ernst & Young
20 enterprise AI solutionsArchitected, supporting $2M in business processes
PMP and CISA certifiedDelivery discipline and audit-ready controls

  1. Find the real workflow

    Start from how the work is done today, with the people who do it, and pick the step where AI removes the most friction.

    Stakeholder workshops in government programmes and at SDU; DealerFlow AI is designed around a workshop's actual booking lifecycle.

  2. Design for control

    The model reads and writes language. Business facts, compliance checks and anything irreversible stay in deterministic code, with a person approving what matters.

    Confirm-before-write bookings in DealerFlow AI; rule-based document checks and recruiter approval in Recruitment AI Ops.

  3. Pilot behind a clean boundary

    Run on safe data first, with a single integration seam so the pilot can be swapped onto the client's real systems without a rewrite.

    DealerFlow's dealership adapter; the n8n and Odoo production mapping in Recruitment AI Ops.

  4. Measure before scaling

    Agree the success measures up front and evaluate against them, not against a demo that looked good.

    HealthProofAI's retrieval, faithfulness and audit metrics; DealerFlow's KPI set, including a 0% duplicate-booking target.

  5. Govern and hand over

    Trace every decision, protect personal data and leave the client a system their team can run, change and audit.

    Blockchain audit trails and EU AI Act and GDPR work in my research; PII-redacted tracing and an Azure rollout plan in DealerFlow; ServiceNow change management.
