## About Me

I am an AI and Software Engineer with a PhD in Engineering and Science (Software Engineering) and more than 12 years of experience spanning software engineering, AI research, university teaching, technical project delivery and public-sector digital transformation.

My research and engineering work covers Large Language Models, Retrieval-Augmented Generation, Agentic AI, Explainable AI, Responsible AI, Natural Language Processing, blockchain-based auditability and production-oriented intelligent systems. I develop trustworthy, interpretable and secure AI solutions for real-world healthcare, cybersecurity and digital-infrastructure applications.
